# Japan–U.S. Global Partnership Grant Program (2026–2027)

- **Funder:** The Japan Foundation, New York
- **Deadline:** December 1, 2025
- **Grant Size:** ~$50K–$100K per year (multi-year possible)
- **Eligibility:** U.S.-based 501(c)(3) organizations partnering with Japanese institutions
- **Cost Share:** Minimum 20% non-Japan Foundation funding required

## Program Focus
- Climate change, sustainability, and disaster resilience
- Digital transformation, AI ethics, and cybersecurity
- Aging populations, health, and social inclusion
- Public health and community resilience

## Relevance to NumFOCUS
This program aligns with NumFOCUS priorities around:
- Open and reproducible scientific computing
- Transparent and ethical AI
- Open data and climate modeling
- International collaboration and knowledge exchange

## Notes
This opportunity supports **symposia, workshops, and exchange-based projects** and is well suited for NumFOCUS projects participating in **international open science collaborations** with Japanese partners.
