# NSF 25-546: NAIRR Operations Center (NAIRR-OC)

**Funder:** NSF, CISE  
**Posted:** Sept 2, 2025  
**Closing Date:** Feb 4, 2026  
**Total Funding:** $35M (single award)

## Program Overview
The NAIRR-OC will build and manage a national AI infrastructure, supporting datasets, models, computing resources, and educational tools for U.S. researchers.

## Core Responsibilities
- Organizational Leadership
- Infrastructure Integration
- Community Development
- Pilot Coordination
- Public–Private Collaboration

## Strategic Focus Areas
| Area | Relevant NumFOCUS Projects | Concept Ideas |
|------|---------------------------|---------------|
| Open & Reproducible AI | scikit-learn, PyMC, SciML, ArviZ | Open AI testing & benchmarking, model interpretability |
| Data Infrastructure & FAIR Access | Xarray, Zarr, pandas, GDAL | Scalable, cloud-native FAIR data workflows |
| AI Transparency & Ethics | Stan, PyMC, QuantEcon | Probabilistic & interpretable AI frameworks |
| Open Scientific Computing | NumPy, SciPy, Julia, conda-forge | Integrate open-source tools with NAIRR data & compute |
| Community & Workforce Development | rOpenSci, Open Journals, Jupyter | Education & documentation frameworks |

## Funding Strategy Fit
- $100K Tier – Integration of open-source AI frameworks  
- $1M+ Tier – Co-develop reproducible AI infrastructure at scale
