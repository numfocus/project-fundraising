# NSF 23-575 Centers for Chemical Innovation (CCI)

## Funding Overview
- Funder: NSF – CHE, MPS
- Phase I: Up to $1.8M / 3 years
- Phase II: Up to $4M / year for 5 years (renewable to 10)

## Key Dates
| Proposal Type | Deadline |
|---------------|---------|
| Phase I Preliminary | Aug 10, 2023 |
| Phase I Full | Feb 7, 2024 |
| Phase II Full | Oct 18, 2023; Oct 22, 2024; Nov 17, 2025 |

## Eligibility
- U.S. IHEs & non-profit organizations
- PI: One only; no co-PIs
- Phase I team: min. 4 senior researchers

## Proposal Highlights
- Center-scale challenge in fundamental chemistry
- Synergy across teams
- Robust leadership & management
- Center-wide data strategy
- Broader impacts (innovation, education, DEI, outreach)
