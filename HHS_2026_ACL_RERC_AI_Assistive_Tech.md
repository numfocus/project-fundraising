# HHS / ACL – Rehabilitation Engineering Research Center (RERC)
## AI-Driven Assistive and Rehabilitation Technologies

- **Funder:** U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS)
- **Agency:** Administration for Community Living (ACL) – NIDILRR
- **Opportunity ID:** HHS-2026-ACL-NIDILRR-REGE-0212
- **Deadline:** March 12, 2026
- **Award Size:** ~$975,000 total (single award)
- **Project Period:** 5 years
- **Eligibility:** U.S. nonprofits, universities, research organizations, small businesses, and government entities

## Focus Areas
- AI- and ML-driven assistive and rehabilitation technologies
- Human-centered and adaptive systems
- Accessibility, ethics, and inclusive design
- Sensor-based rehabilitation and performance monitoring

## Relevance to NumFOCUS
This opportunity aligns with NumFOCUS projects focused on:
- Interpretable and reproducible machine learning
- Scientific computing for accessibility and rehabilitation research
- Ethical and transparent AI systems
- Open-source tools supporting assistive technologies

## Notes
This RFP is well suited for NumFOCUS projects to participate as **technical or open-source partners** within a larger academic or clinical-led consortium.
